- **D-051 — Conversation lines are recorded into the history (milestone 1.6, your request)** · 2026-10-09 · accepted · Reverses item (3) of D-050. Generated lines are entered as a logged input (`Command::RecordDialogue`) so they are part of saves, replays and hashes, and they are attached to the residents' memory of the conversation (`Memory.talk` with start tick, tone, turns and lines; `Talk.lines` while it is under way), so a conversation can be recalled later (`conversation::recall`, shown by `pg residents inspect`). Only what the AI actually produced is stored; fallback lines are rebuilt from the data on recall. What is recorded is what was shown (fallback for the turns already spoken when the reply arrived). Outcomes still never depend on the words: a test compares runs with AI on, off and failing on everything but the words. Costs: schema 4 (unshipped) gained those fields, so the shape file, fixtures and pins were re-recorded; saved state grows by the generated lines only when AI is on.
- **D-050 — AI lines for conversations someone can hear (milestone 1.5)** · 2026-10-08 · accepted · Blueprint section 9.4 and 10.4 as built. (1) **Pure parts in `pg-ai::dialogue`:** a prompt builder (trusted instructions in the system part, every name, mood and memory summary only as cleaned, bounded data in the user part) and a reply parser that keeps exactly one checked line per turn or refuses (link, over-long, empty, out of character, content filter for cozy worlds and the device filter). (2) **`pg-runtime::dialogue::DialogueService`** watches `conversation.started`; only when the focused resident is within `hear_range` of a speaker does it prepare fallback lines at once and, with AI on, ask the client on the worker pool: one request per conversation, a cooldown per resident (three game hours), at most two in flight, plus the client's rate cap, cache and breaker. A reply replaces only the turns not yet spoken; late, refused or failed replies change nothing. (3) **Deviation:** the Blueprint says generated lines are recorded as a `Dialogue` input. They are not: the text never feeds state, so it is kept in a bounded runtime store (not in saves, replays or hashes), which also makes outcomes identical with AI on, off or failing by construction (tested by hash). Persisting lines with the conversation record can come with the 1.7 history views if wanted. (4) **Focus:** `AppController::set_dialogue_focus`; nothing sets it until the renderer and inspector (1.6, 1.7) exist, so no AI request is made yet in the shipped app. (5) **Tools and lint:** `pg ai dialogue-test` (prompt, fallback lines, test a reply, no network); content lint now warns about over-long dialogue lines, placeholders other than name and other, topics with nothing to say and unreachable tones (S-062). Fallback lines walk the line set from a seeded start so none repeats within a talk, and address people by first name. **Not done in 1.5 (moved):** S-054 remember-when callbacks and S-057 per-turn tone with relationship and mood bands, which want the renderer's bubbles to judge by; they move to 1.6/1.7.

- **D-043 — Choices made in 1.2 (town generator) and pawn-aware routing moved up** · 2026-10-08 · accepted · Blueprint v3.4 (section 12.1 and 7.3). (1) **Buildings stand along streets** rather than being cut from lots in blocks: a sidewalk, a yard tile that is the entrance, then the footprint, with roles from each district's weights (`worldgen.json`). - **D-042 — Choices made in 1.1 (needs, mood and capacities)** · 2026-10-08 · accepted · Blueprint §8.1, §8.2 (to be folded into the Blueprint at the 1.2 checkpoint). (1) **Needs are data-driven and extensible.** Each need in `needs.json` has its decay (points per game hour, scaled while walking), thresholds, the capacities it lowers when urgent or critical, an optional passive recovery for a pawn that cannot act, and a `restore` block (the action, the place kind, how long, and the day's regular times). Decay is computed from the global minute counter (`per_minute`), so 55 points an hour is exactly 55 over any sixty minutes with nothing to carry or save. (2) **Restoring is an attribute of actions.** `ActionDef.restores` lists the needs an action restores while it is performed (and whether another pawn must be near); the new built-in actions are `eat`, `sleep`, `rest` and `socialise`. Hunger and energy are restored at home; social at a gathering place (the plaza, until 1.2 generates real ones). (3) **The day has a rhythm.** Regular meals, bedtime and company come from `restore.routine` windows, placed by the scheduler as urgent-need reservations (new `UrgentNeed.earliest` bound); a need that falls below urgent adds an unplanned one at once and asks for a replan. The earlier "predicted to become urgent" placement put every meal at midnight; the sentence for `urgent_need_placed` changed to match. (4) **Capacities are real state** (S-040): six permille values per pawn, derived each minute from needs, read by movement (speed scales with `moving`; below the consciousness threshold a pawn neither walks nor starts tasks) and by the task planner. A pawn that is exhausted collapses where it stands, recovers slowly and stays down until its energy is back at the urgent level (no flicker at the threshold). The organism system takes over as the producer in Stage 2B. (5) **Mood** follows D-038: the ordered rule table from `mood.json` is evaluated each minute; memories made before the world began (tick 0) do not move mood. `idle_since` on the pawn supports the `bored` rule. (6) **Schema 4 grew** (still in development, so no new schema number): pawns gained `capacities`, `home_tile` and `idle_since`; the migration, the v4 fixture and the v3 fixture's recorded hash were refreshed. (7) **Installation.** Content with game data installs the needs and mood systems and the plan source that knows about needs when it is given to a `Sim` (`Sim::with_content`), replacing the scaffolding probe, so every tool that loads the base pack gets them. (8) **Known limit, scheduled in 1.3:** crowds at one gathering place jam without pawn-aware routing (S-018); on open ground four residents look after themselves for days, and on the generated dev town and with more residents social contact sometimes fails. The integration test says so and uses open ground. (9) New events (`need.urgent`, `need.critical`, `pawn.collapsed`, `pawn.recovered`, `mood.changed`, category `life`) and `pg sim --needs`.
